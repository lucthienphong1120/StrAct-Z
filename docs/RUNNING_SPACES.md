# 🌟 Premium Running Urban Spaces & Landscapes - StrAct Z

This document outlines the evaluation criteria for high-quality running spaces in Hanoi and lists premier runner-approved urban areas, parks, and lakes with their respective layout dimensions and size profiles.

---

## 📐 High-Quality Running Urban Space Criteria

An urban development or open residential space is classified as a premium running zone when it satisfies the following core criteria:

1.  **Continuous Route Length (Chiều dài cung đường đủ lớn):**
    *   The estate or park layout must support continuous routes of 2 km to 5 km (or more) without forcing runners into frequent U-turns or highly repetitive, short loop counts.
2.  **Safe Internal Traffic Infrastructure (Hạ tầng giao thông nội khu an toàn):**
    *   Features wide, paved, unobstructed pedestrian sidewalks, or ideally dedicated running tracks.
    *   Strict limits or complete segregation from external through-traffic (especially heavy passenger or commercial vehicles), with minimal major junctions or frequent traffic signals.
3.  **Low Construction Density & Green Cover (Mật độ xây dựng thấp & nhiều mảng xanh):**
    *   Must feature central parks, water-body buffers (lakes, ponds), and high tree canopy coverage to reduce the urban heat island effect and maintain clean air during peak summer runs.
4.  **Security & Connectivity (Không gian mở kết nối):**
    *   Equipped with professional night lighting and active 24/7 security patrols to ensure absolute safety for early morning and late night runner sessions.

## 🏢 Premier Large-Scale Running Estates (Estates over 2,000 m²)

The following developments represent the highest-tier running environments in Hanoi, ranked by total spatial footprint:

### 1. Vinhomes Ocean Park (Gia Lâm)
*   **Total Footprint:** ~4,200,000 m² (420 ha)
*   **Running Profile:** Contains Hanoi's largest artificial saltwater beach lagoon and central freshwater lakes. Extremely long, open roads make this the premier destination for long runs (10 km to 21 km Half-Marathons) without route repetition.

### 2. Khu đô thị Ciputra (Nam Thăng Long - Tây Hồ / Bắc Từ Liêm)
*   **Total Footprint:** ~3,010,000 m² (301 ha)
*   **Running Profile:** A long-standing "running sanctuary" in Hanoi. Offers quiet, tree-canopied paths, exceptionally low traffic density, and clean air due to its immediate proximity to the Red River (Sông Hồng) and West Lake (Hồ Tây).

### 3. Vinhomes Smart City (Nam Từ Liêm)
*   **Total Footprint:** ~2,800,000 m² (280 ha)
*   **Running Profile:** Centered around a massive sports and lifestyle park. Roads feature clear division markings and extremely flat pavement, supporting high-tempo workouts.

### 4. Khu đô thị Vinhomes Riverside (Long Biên)
*   **Total Footprint:** ~2,800,000 m² (280 ha across Phase 1 & 2)
*   **Running Profile:** Features a Venice-style canal network wrapping around residential villas. The roads are highly secluded, quiet, and shaded, offering cool temperatures year-round.

## 🏞️ Major Natural Water Bodies & Linear Parks

*   **Hồ Tây (West Lake):** ~5,260,000 m² (526 ha) of water surface area. The perimeter route is ~15-17 km, serving as Hanoi's primary long run track.
*   **Công viên Yên Sở:** ~3,230,000 m² (323 ha) of total park and lake landscape. High green ratio and zero motor vehicles make it ideal for traffic-free training.
*   **Hồ Hoàn Kiếm (Hồ Gươm):** ~120,000 m² (12 ha) water surface. Circle loop of ~1.7 km, highly popular for early morning weekend runs.
*   **Đê Sông Hồng (Red River Dike):** A linear scenic running corridor along the dikes of the Red River, popular for uninterrupted long runs.
