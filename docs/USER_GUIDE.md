# 📖 User Guide

Welcome to StrAct Z! This guide explains how to use the dashboard to configure and automate your Strava activities.

## 1. Connecting Strava

Before you can upload activities, you must link your Strava account.
- Look for the **🔗 Strava Account** card on the right side of the dashboard.
- Click **Connect with Strava**.
- You will be redirected to Strava to authorize the application. Once approved, you'll be brought back to the dashboard, and your account name and avatar will appear.
- *Note: In a multi-user environment, your Strava tokens are strictly isolated to your StrAct Z login.*

## 2. Route Configuration

The **🗺️ Route Configuration** section defines where and how routes are generated.

- **Allowed Districts:** Select which districts in Hanoi the route can originate and travel through. 12 inner-city districts are available.
- **Max Districts per Route:** Defines how far the route can spread. Max 2 is recommended for realistic urban running.
- **Use OSRM:** When enabled, the route will accurately snap to real roads and paths. If disabled (or if OSRM is offline), the system falls back to drawing paths across the map.

### Time Configuration
- **Random Time Bounds:** If an activity is generated, its start time will fall between these bounds.
- **Avoid Workhours:** Prevent activities from being generated during typical office hours to maintain realism.
- **Target Date & Custom Time:** You can toggle "Custom Time (GPX Only)" to explicitly set the date and time for a manual GPX generation. This disables the random time logic.

## 3. Activity Settings & Simulation

The **⚙️ Activity Settings** panel controls the biometrics and realism of the run.

- **Activity Type:** Choose Run, Walk, Ride, or Random.
- **Heart Rate Data:** Define the Minimum and Maximum heart rate. The engine will simulate exertion curves within this range.
- **🌤️ Weather Sim:** When enabled, there is a 30% chance the activity will simulate "hot weather", artificially raising the overall heart rate by 3 to 8 BPM.
- **🚦 Red Lights:** When enabled, the runner has a small chance (1.5% per GPS point) to stop for 15-60 seconds at an intersection, causing the pace to drop to zero and the heart rate to temporarily recover.

*Always remember to click the **💾 Save** button after making changes.*

## 4. Manual Generation vs Auto Schedule

### Manual Actions
Use the buttons at the top of the dashboard:
- **📝 Generate GPX Only:** Creates an activity and saves the GPX file locally (you can download it from the Local History table). It does not upload to Strava.
- **🚀 Generate & Upload:** Generates a new activity and immediately pushes it to Strava.

### Auto Schedule
The **⏰ Auto Schedule** card allows you to put generation on autopilot.
- Toggle the switch to enable it.
- **Daily Run Time:** The time (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh timezone) the server will wake up to generate activities.
- **Min/Max Count:** The number of activities to generate when the schedule runs (e.g., 1 to 2). Note: To prevent API bans, the system hard-caps uploads to 2 per day.

## 5. History and Management
Scroll down to the **Local Generated History** table.
- Here you can view all activities generated by your account.
- You can **Upload** pending activities, **Download** the raw GPX files, or **Delete** activities (which removes them from the local DB and optionally attempts to delete them from Strava).
